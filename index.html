How FoodWise works

A simple path from extra food to a meaningful meal, with people reviewing safe handling and collection.

🍲

1. List surplus food

Donors add food details, quantity, allergens, storage information and a collection deadline.

🤝

2. Connect with a receiver

Eligible community organizations discover available listings and request the quantity they need.

📍

3. Collect and track

Follow request status, confirm collection and update the community impact record.
